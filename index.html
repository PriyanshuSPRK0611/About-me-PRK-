01 / ABOUT ME
PRK—001

I was never very comfortable with ordinary answers.

Hi, I’m Priyanshu Ranjan Karn. People also know me as PRK.

I was born in Purnia, Bihar, India, in a middle-class family. But I never really felt like a person with middle-of-the-road thoughts.

Since childhood, there has always been a small machine running inside my head — asking why? How? What?

Why does something happen the way it does? How does it actually work? What happens if we change it? And what happens if we look at it completely differently?

Those questions have followed me through life. And strangely enough, I don't see them as a problem. I see them as a direction.

Because every question opens another door. And behind some of those doors, I have discovered ideas, technologies, people, systems and ways of looking at the world that I might never have found otherwise.

I want to understand the world — not simply live inside it.

Technology is one of the biggest reasons for that curiosity.

I believe technology is not just another industry. It can completely change the way humanity sees the world. It can redefine how we communicate, build, learn, create and solve problems.

I am especially fascinated by engineering, coding, artificial intelligence, machine learning and the systems behind modern technology.

I believe that with enough understanding of technology, we may not simply react to the future — we may be able to anticipate parts of it and help build what comes next.

But technology isn't the only force that interests me.

Politics changes things too.

Politics can influence technology, business, food, people, economies, ideas, borders and even the way societies think.

That is why I am deeply interested in politics, geopolitics, diplomacy and debate.

I love asking questions. I love debating. I love hearing an argument that challenges my own thinking.

Because sometimes the most useful person in a room is not the person saying “yes”. It is the person asking, “But why?”

I am also interested in editing, documentaries, storytelling and anything that makes me look at an ordinary subject from an unusual angle.

Maybe I am still figuring out exactly where all of this leads.

But perhaps that is the point.

I don't want a finished map. I want to help draw one.

03 / VISION

Technology changes what is possible. Politics changes what is allowed. Business changes what becomes real.

+

I believe these three forces — technology, politics and business — can shape almost everything around us.

Technology gives humanity new capabilities. Politics decides how societies use power, resources and ideas. Business takes ideas from imagination into the real world.

Put them together and something much bigger becomes possible.

My ambition is not simply to become good at one of them.

I want to understand the intersection.

Because the biggest changes rarely belong to one field alone. They happen when different worlds collide.

A piece of technology can become a business. A business can influence society. Politics can accelerate or restrict it. And society can demand something completely new in return.

That cycle is where I want to learn, experiment and eventually build.

04 / THE TEAM

Different minds. Better possibilities.

I don't believe great things are built by everyone thinking the same way.

I believe they are built when different minds collide.

The positive thinker. The negative thinker. The person who doubts everything. The person who keeps asking questions. The person who sees a problem. The person who sees an opportunity.

These are the people through whom I believe anything can be reshaped, redesigned and improved.

I love bringing people together from different backgrounds, different skills, different fields and completely different perspectives of the world.

I am always searching for the right people around me — not simply to make my circle bigger, but to make it more powerful, meaningful and capable of creating something that none of us could have created alone.

Different minds don't weaken an idea. Sometimes, they are exactly what makes it stronger.
