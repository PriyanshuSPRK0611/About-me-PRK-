01 — ABOUT ME
PERSONAL / 001
01

I don't really fit into boxes.

Hi, I am Priyanshu Ranjan Karn. People also know me as PRK.

I was born in Purnia, Bihar, India, in a middle-class family. But being middle-class never meant having middle-sized thoughts.

Since childhood, my mind has been unusually interested in three simple questions: WHY? HOW? WHAT IF?

Those questions have taken me into technology, politics, business, editing, documentaries, conversations and countless ideas that probably should have stayed inside my head. Fortunately, they didn't.

I am fascinated by technology because I believe it does more than make life convenient. It changes the way people think, communicate, build and see the world.

I am especially interested in coding, artificial intelligence, machine learning and engineering. Not because technology is automatically the answer to everything— but because finding better questions usually starts there.

Politics interests me for a completely different reason. Politics decides how ideas move through society. It influences technology, business, people, resources, opinions and sometimes even what becomes possible.

I like debating. I like questioning. I like conversations where nobody is completely sure who is right. Because sometimes the best idea is the one that survives the argument.

I also enjoy editing, documentaries, discovering unusual ideas and learning from people who see the world differently.

I am not trying to have all the answers. I am more interested in becoming the person who keeps asking better questions.

03 — VISION
DIRECTION / 003
03

Technology.
Politics.
Business.

I believe these three forces have an enormous influence on the shape of the future.

Technology changes what humans can do. Politics influences what societies choose to do. Business decides, in many cases, what actually gets built.

Put them together and you get something much larger than a career or a company. You get the machinery that can reshape the world around us.

My vision is to explore the intersection of these three areas and understand how they can be used to create better systems, better technology and better possibilities for the future.

04 — THE TEAM
PEOPLE / 004
04

Different minds.

I don't believe great things are built by everyone thinking the same way.

I want to bring together people with completely different ways of looking at the world.

The positive thinker. The negative thinker. The person who doubts everything. The person who keeps asking uncomfortable questions.

Builders, thinkers, creators, engineers, designers, strategists and people who simply see something differently.

Different skills are useful. Different perspectives are even more useful.

The goal is not to create a room where everyone agrees. The goal is to create a room where ideas become stronger because they are challenged.
