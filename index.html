ALSO KNOWN AS PRK

I was born in Purnia, Bihar, India, in a middle-class family. But being middle-class never meant having middle-sized thoughts.

Since childhood, my mind has been unusually interested in three simple questions:

WHY? HOW? WHAT IF?

Those questions have taken me into technology, politics, business, editing, documentaries, conversations and countless ideas that probably should have stayed inside my head. Fortunately, they didn't.

I am fascinated by technology because I believe it does more than make life convenient. It changes the way people think, communicate, build and see the world.

I am especially interested in coding, artificial intelligence, machine learning and engineering.

Not because technology is automatically the answer to everything — but because finding better questions usually starts there.

Politics interests me for a completely different reason.

Politics decides how ideas move through society. It influences technology, business, people, resources, opinions and sometimes even what becomes possible.

I like debating. I like questioning. I like conversations where nobody is completely sure who is right.

Because sometimes the best idea is the one that survives the argument.

I also enjoy editing, documentaries, discovering unusual ideas and learning from people who see the world differently.

I don't believe curiosity is a hobby. I think it is a way of looking at the world.

And honestly, I would rather spend my time asking an uncomfortable question than accepting a comfortable answer.

I am not trying to have all the answers. I am more interested in becoming the person who keeps asking better questions.

02 — WHAT DRIVES ME

What drives me. What keeps me moving.

I have always been driven by curiosity. The need to understand why something happens, how it works, and what could happen if we looked at it differently.

Since childhood, I have had things constantly going around in my mind — why, how, what, and sometimes questions that probably did not need to be asked in the first place.

Technology is one of the biggest things that drives me. I believe technology is the future and has the power to change the way we see the world. It can redefine how people live, communicate, build and solve problems.

I am especially interested in engineering, coding, artificial intelligence and machine learning. I believe these technologies can become powerful tools for solving some of the world's biggest problems.

But technology is not the only thing that interests me.

Politics changes the shape of everything.

Politics can influence technology, business, food, people, resources, opinions, ideas and almost everything around us. That is why I am very interested in politics, diplomacy and the way decisions shape society.

I love debating. I love asking questions. I keep asking questions because I believe that sometimes the best way to understand something is to challenge it.

I am also interested in editing, watching documentaries that inspire me, discovering unusual ideas, having meaningful conversations and learning from people who see the world differently.

I don't believe curiosity is just a hobby. I believe it is a way of looking at the world.

And honestly, I would rather spend my time asking an uncomfortable question than accepting a comfortable answer.

I am not trying to have all the answers. I am more interested in becoming the person who keeps asking better questions.
