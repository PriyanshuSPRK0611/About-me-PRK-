01 · THE BEGINNING

Why We
Created It

The world is changing extremely quickly.

Artificial intelligence, science, technology, politics, business, gaming, sports, fitness and society are constantly evolving. Asia, in particular, is becoming an increasingly important part of the global future.

I wanted to create a platform where people could explore the important things happening around us — while also thinking about where those developments could lead us in the future.

That idea became Future Asia Journal.
