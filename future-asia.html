Why Asia

The center of gravity moved. The newsroom should follow.

Asia is not a beat. It is the main event. Nearly six in ten people alive live here. More than half the world’s internet users log on here. This decade, the region is expected to drive the majority of global growth. Yet global coverage of Asia too often happens from somewhere else, framed for somewhere else. We are building the newsroom Asia has earned — based here, sourced here, obsessed with here.

The Scale

Decisions made here reach every timezone by breakfast.

A rate decision in Tokyo, a fab opening in Taiwan, an election in the world’s largest democracy — none of it stays regional for long. Covering Asia well is not regional interest. It is world news, reported at the source.

The Gap

A continent this consequential deserves a permanent newsroom.

Global coverage of Asia spikes for emergencies and election weeks, then moves on. The daily texture of the region — the policy fights, the breakthroughs, the culture shifting in real time — goes underreported. That gap is our opening, and our obligation.

The Byline

The best coverage of a place is written by people of it.

Our reporters live in the cities they cover. They speak the languages, read the local papers, and know which sources hold up. For too long the story of Asia carried someone else’s byline. We are changing that — permanently.

Our Standard

Twice-checked.
Before it’s true.

Speed means nothing without trust. Every story at Future Asia Journal passes two independent verifications — sources confirmed, documents checked, claims cross-examined by a second editor — before it reaches you. If it cannot be verified twice, it does not run. That is the entire rulebook.
