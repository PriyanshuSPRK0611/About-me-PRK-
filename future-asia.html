01 · The Beginning

Why We Created It

The world is changing extremely quickly.

Artificial intelligence, science, technology, politics, business, gaming, sports, fitness and society are constantly evolving. Asia, in particular, is becoming an increasingly important part of the global future.

I wanted to create a platform where people could explore the important things happening around us — while also thinking about where those developments could lead us in the future.

That idea became Future Asia Journal.

02 · The Platform

What Is Future Asia Journal?

Future Asia Journal is planned as a modern digital publication covering a wide range of subjects.

Artificial Intelligence

Exploring AI, emerging technologies and how intelligent systems may change the future.

Technology

Following important developments in technology and the digital world.

Science

Discovering scientific developments and ideas that can shape tomorrow.

Politics & Business

Understanding major political, economic and business developments.

Gaming & Sports

Covering entertainment, gaming, sports and the communities around them.

Society & The World

Exploring important developments across Asia and the wider world.

03 · The Vision

More Than A News Website

The goal isn't simply to create another website that publishes articles.

I want Future Asia Journal to become a platform where people can understand what is happening, ask why it is happening, and think about what could happen next.

“Understand today. Explore tomorrow. Build the future.”

04 · Learning By Building

This Project Is Also My Classroom

Future Asia Journal is not only a website I want to launch. It is also one of the biggest learning projects in my journey into technology.

I'm learning how an idea becomes a real digital product.

Web Development

Learning how modern websites are designed and developed.

Programming

Building my understanding of coding and software development.

Databases

Learning how information can be stored, organized and managed.

AI & Technology

Exploring how artificial intelligence can become part of future products.

05 · Current Progress

What We Have Built So Far

The first stage has been about creating the foundation.

The website structure and visual identity have been started, and the project is now being prepared for the systems that will eventually power the publication.

This includes understanding how the different parts of a modern digital platform work together:

Frontend → Backend → Database → Dashboard → Content → Deployment

The current version is therefore not the final version.

It is the beginning.

06 · What's Pending

What Still Needs To Be Built?

Database

A proper database needs to store articles, categories, images and other platform information.

Admin Dashboard

A private dashboard where articles and other content can be created, edited, organized and published.

Content Management

Better systems for managing headlines, articles, images, categories, featured stories and publication status.

Design & Experience

Continuing to improve the website so it is clean, fast, responsive and enjoyable to use.

SEO & Discoverability

Improving the site's structure so useful original stories can be discovered more easily.

Security & Reliability

Building the systems needed to make the platform reliable, secure and ready to grow.

08 · Why Asia?

Looking Toward Asia

Asia is one of the most interesting places to watch when thinking about the future.

It contains enormous populations, rapidly developing technologies, major economies, different political systems, diverse cultures and some of the world's most ambitious technology ecosystems.

Future Asia Journal is about being curious and trying to understand different countries, people, technologies, ideas and developments.

Not just looking at today's headlines — but thinking beyond them.

09 · The Bigger Picture

One Project. A Bigger Journey.

Future Asia Journal is one project, but it represents something much bigger for me.

I want to learn how technology can turn ideas into real things. I want to build software, explore artificial intelligence, work with curious people and eventually create a team capable of building much larger ideas.

Future Asia Journal is one of the first steps toward that future.

The foundation is being built today for something that may become much bigger tomorrow.

10 · Join The Journey

There are unfinished systems, ideas that haven't been implemented, features that still need to be designed and a lot that I still need to learn.

But that's exactly where the journey begins.

Build the foundation. Improve it. Grow it. Then see what it can become.
